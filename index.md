A research journal about building a better lossless compressor. The goal is a complete program and archive that recover Wikipedia exactly while beating the Hutter Prize or Large Text Compression Benchmark record.

These essays follow the questions behind the experiments: what makes a model worth its storage cost, when a measurement deserves trust, and which results should change the allocation of research effort.
