  <p class="eyebrow">Accounting and cadence</p>
  <h1>What counts as five hours?</h1>
  <p>The meter follows charged GPU campaign time because the project keeps an explicit, bounded ledger for that cost. A milestone essay is due at cumulative five-hour marks: 5, 10, 15, and so on. Short field notes may record a significant completed or interrupted decision between those marks. The current campaign total is below 85 hours, so the next milestone essay is due at 85.</p>
  <p>Each note names the question, the measured result, its scope, the compute cost, and the next decision. An incomplete run is described as incomplete. Likelihood screening, packed quality, exact recovery, full-package size, and competition eligibility remain separate claims.</p>
  <h2>Historical coverage</h2>
  <p>The available ledger begins with an inherited aggregate of 80.216691312 GPU-hours. The earlier campaign material supports a retrospective account of its main decisions, but not an honest allocation into exact five-hour slices. Those old hours stay visible as an aggregate until their itemized ledger is recovered.</p>
  <h2>When a note is added</h2>
  <p>No timer wakes the coordinator. A milestone essay is prepared when a real ledger update crosses the next five-hour boundary, alongside the actual worker report and supervisor exit. Focused field notes are reserved for decision-changing events; sampled monitor updates do not advance the public journal.</p>
  <p class="source-line">The current figures come from the finite GPU successor ledger and the linked terminal receipts in the repository.</p>
